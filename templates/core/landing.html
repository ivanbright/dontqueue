FAQ

We have the answers

General

What makes {{ PLATFORM_NAME }} different from a normal ticketing tool?

It’s built for decisions, not just labels. Instead of only handing out numbers, it explains what’s happening in your lobby — what changed, how long people are waiting, and what to review next.

Who is {{ PLATFORM_NAME }} for?

Clinics, banks, salons and service teams that want clear visibility into wait times, walk-ins and what’s driving busy periods — especially when the front desk is shared.

Does {{ PLATFORM_NAME }} replace my front desk?

No. It complements your team by keeping the queue clean and current, so check-in is faster and customers stop asking “how long”.

Wait estimates

How are wait times calculated?

Every estimate blends observed service history with the number of active providers, and is clearly labelled approximate so nobody is promised a false precision.

What happens if a customer misses their turn?

After a configurable grace period a turn is marked missed. Depending on your rules, the customer can be returned to the waiting list in one tap — and it’s all recorded.

Setup & integrations

How long does setup take?

Create an organization, add a branch and a service, and you have a live queue in minutes. Customers can join from a link or at the desk immediately.

Security and privacy

Is my data isolated from other organizations?

Yes. Every branch, service and customer belongs to a single organization, and access is enforced on the server for every request — never by hiding a button.
